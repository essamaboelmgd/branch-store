# BRANCH storefront

## Surface and mode

`/` — Persuade. The public homepage should help a first-time buyer understand BRANCH, find a wood board, and move into browsing or comparison.

## Audience and action

Arabic-speaking buyers in Egypt who need board material for a project. They should be able to compare sample offers by material, dimensions, unit price, availability, and supplier, then browse without creating an account.

## Proof and content

Use labeled demonstration offers, sample suppliers, and product details. Show price per board and dimensions in the first product views. Do not imply the sample prices or companies are verified offers.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

Arabic-first RTL, BRANCH wordmark, static frontend, no real backend. The user supplied Figma node `1:829` as a storefront-shape reference; replace its electronics content and imagery with wood-marketplace content. Supplier approval, delivery/address requirements, and multi-supplier request splitting remain open.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make board offers comparable by showing supplier, material, dimensions, stock, price, and unit together. Refuse a hero that makes a cheap-wood claim without an actual sample offer.

OWN-WORLD: Translate an Egyptian architectural material schedule into storefront language: measured dimension lines, board samples, and clear offer stamps on a pale canvas, with forest-green structure and a clay-orange action color. Keep the open hierarchy of the supplied Figma storefront.

STORY: A buyer recognizes BRANCH as a multi-supplier board marketplace, scans sample materials, opens an offer, compares it, and requests it as a guest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep a compact header and search above a wide split hero. Place the Arabic message, product search, and browse action on the RTL reading side; show a locally authored board stack with a labeled sample price and unit on the other side.

FORM: Use the supplied Figma storefront rhythm, adapted to wood, with architectural material schedules as grounded candidate 6 of 7. Seed `d80a560f`; the roll returned no challengers, so the user's pinned Figma structure controls. Code-led build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
