# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React, Vite, and TypeScript with plain CSS; produce a static frontend build.

## Users

Buyers in the Egyptian market who need to find, compare, and request wood boards without creating an account. Supplier companies use a separate portal to present inventory and review purchase requests.

## Product Purpose

BRANCH brings independent wood and board suppliers into one marketplace. Buyers can compare materials, dimensions, prices, units, availability, and suppliers, then submit a guest purchase request. Each supplier reviews requests for its own products.

## Positioning

BRANCH is a multi-company marketplace for wood boards that makes supplier offers easier to discover and compare, while leaving the supplier responsible for accepting or rejecting a request.

## Operating Context

Buyers browse public listings, inspect supplier and product details, compare offers, add quantities to a cart, and submit contact details as a guest. Supplier representatives register or log in, manage company information and products, and review requests addressed to their company.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current deliverable is a frontend prototype with realistic mock data and mock state.
- The frontend must build to static files and work without a backend or database.
- The buyer flow includes product discovery, product details, comparison, cart, guest checkout, and order confirmation.
- Supplier views include registration, login, dashboard, product management, order management, and company settings.
- Supplier order states in scope are Pending, Accepted, and Rejected. A submitted request is not automatically accepted.
- MDF, HDF, plywood, and similar materials are examples only, not a finalized category taxonomy.
- Supplier approval, delivery/address requirements, and the handling of carts containing products from multiple suppliers remain undecided.
- Backend services, payments, commissions, shipping, advanced inventory, complex permissions, and a BRANCH administration panel are outside this stage.

## Brand Commitments

The brand name is BRANCH. The first interface should use Arabic and right-to-left layout, with the English brand name preserved and room for later English support. The requested product character is modern, friendly, clean, simple, trustworthy, and professional for businesses. Final colors, fonts, and logo are not approved. The supplied Figma store is a visual reference for storefront structure, not a source for electronics content or imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

The product and frontend scope come from the user-provided BRANCH brief. No approved logo, product photography, supplier list, customer quotes, or real price data were supplied. Any populated companies, inventory, prices, and orders in the prototype must read as sample data rather than verified market offers.

## Product Principles

- Show the supplier, price, price unit, material, dimensions, and availability before a buyer submits a request.
- Let buyers explore and request products without an account.
- Keep supplier companies, their products, and their order decisions separate.
- Make the storefront easy to browse while keeping supplier administration clear and practical.
